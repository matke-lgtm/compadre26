Food & The Pace of the Trip

Two instincts in this group: cook every meal at the house, or let the club serve us. Both are good and we can have both without arguing about it on Thursday night. The plan below splits it — one pickup night, one house dinner, one served dinner — and everything golf-adjacent (tacos, box lunches, men's grill) comes from the club so nobody is cooking before a round.

Handled by the club

Breakfast tacos in the clubhouse before each round, coffee to go from the men's grill.

Box lunches ordered the morning of and delivered anywhere on property — Metzger Market between 8 and 9 has tables if we want to eat out there.

Lunch in the men's grill between rounds is the easier version, and we have the time for it.

Coffee is handled: Keurig in the house and in every room, a drip maker if someone wants to bring a fancy roast, and coffee to go from the men's grill with the tacos.

The Games

Four rounds, four formats, one running total. Everyone partners everyone by Saturday afternoon, and no single bad round puts anybody out of it. Daniel keeps the card and reads the standings at dinner. All matches played at handicap, 100% of the difference off the low man.

Round 1 · Fri AMFour-Ball Match3 points available each

Two vs. two; match play; best net ball of the partners on every hole. Pairings decided by whoever wins Thursday night's putting. 3 points to each man on the winning side, 1.5 each if the match is halved. Press rules: none. Keep it simple on day one.

Round 2 · Fri PMSixes3 points available each

Three six-hole matches with rotating partners: holes 1–6, 7–12, 13–18. Every man partners every other man once. Each six-hole segment is its own best net ball match. 1 point to each winning partner per segment, 0.5 each if halved. 

Round 3 · Sat AMSingles4 points available each

Two head-to-head matches, net. The Friday points leader picks his opponent; the other two play each other. 3 points for winning your match, 1.5 for a half, plus 1 bonus point to the low net score of the whole group. This is the round where somebody in last place can climb back in.

Round 4 · Sat PMWolf3 points to the winner

Rotating Wolf order off every tee. The Wolf picks a partner after watching the other three drive, or goes Lone Wolf for double, or declares Blind Wolf before anyone hits for triple. Hole values in wolf points; keep the tally on the card. At the end of the round the wolf standings convert to Cup points: 3 for first, 2 for second, 1 for third. Best format to finish on — a good closing stretch can flip the whole trip.
